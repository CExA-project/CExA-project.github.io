Antoine Kempf is a PhD student in high-performance computing at CEA, working on asynchronous algorithms for the neutron transport equation on heterogeneous architectures. Background in mathematics, computer science and high-performance computing through the EUMaster4HPC program (Sorbonne Université, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg), with research internships at RIKEN R-CCS. Interested in performance portability, sparse linear algebra, multigrid methods (and more).
